## Research Documents

### HVAC and Environmental Control
- **[hvac-specifications.md](./hvac-specifications.md)** - Temperature, humidity, and air quality requirements by sport type
  - Basketball/Volleyball: 65-70°F, 40-60% RH
  - Indoor Tennis: 55-62°F winter, low air velocity for ball control
  - Ice Rinks: 30% RH max, fog prevention systems
  - Natatoriums: 80-84°F air, 2-4°F above water temperature
