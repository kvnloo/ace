## 🎾 Facility Overview

### Complete Project Scope
The facility is an **autonomous, multi-floor racketsports complex** in Naperville, Illinois, featuring:
- **24 Tennis Courts**: Grass (6), Hard (6), Clay (6), Wood courts
- **16 Badminton Courts**
- **8 Pickleball Courts**
- **4 Squash Courts**
- **16 Table Tennis Tables**
- **1 Real Tennis Court**
- **Vertical Farming Area**: 2,000 m² (4 sections × 500 m²) on dedicated floor

### Multi-Floor Layout

#### Multi-Floor Layout Overview
- **Ground Floor**: 24 Tennis Courts (grass, hard, clay, wood), Pro Shop, Locker Rooms
- **First Floor**: 16 Badminton Courts, 4 Squash Courts, 16 Table Tennis Tables
- **Second Floor**: 8 Pickleball Courts, 1 Real Tennis Court
- **Third Floor**: 2,000 m² Vertical Farming Area

**Detailed court specifications**: See [tennis-court-specifications.md](tennis-court-specifications.md) for complete dimensions, clearances, and technical requirements for all court types.

#### Third Floor
- **Vertical Farming Area**: 2,000 m² total
  - 4 sections of 500 m² each
  - Dedicated grass cultivation for tennis court maintenance
  - 4ft × 4ft grass patch production
  - Automated hydroponics and climate control

## 🤖 Autonomous Systems Architecture

### Digital Twin Framework
**Integration**: OpenTwins + Voyager + Eureka methodologies

#### OpenTwins Microservices Architecture
- **Eclipse Ditto**: Digital twin definitions and current states
- **Eclipse Hono**: IoT data ingestion via multiple protocols
- **MongoDB**: Digital twin state storage, policies, connections
- **InfluxDB v2**: Time-series data optimization
- **Telegraf**: Metrics collection and integration

#### Voyager Embodied Agents
**Inspired by**: [Voyager MineDojo Research](https://voyager.minedojo.org/)

**Capabilities**:
- **Automatic Curriculum**: Agents propose tasks based on facility state
- **Skill Library**: Executable code for complex maintenance behaviors
- **Iterative Prompting**: Environment feedback and self-verification
- **Applications**: Court maintenance scheduling, grass patch replacement, autonomous cleaning

#### Eureka Reward Design
**Inspired by**: [Eureka Research](https://eureka-research.github.io/)

**Methods**:
- **Zero-Shot Generation**: LLM-generated reward functions
- **Evolutionary Optimization**: Iterative reward refinement
- **Human Feedback Integration**: Safety and quality validation
- **Applications**: Training robots for court maintenance, optimizing resource management

### Autonomous Features

#### Court Maintenance
- **Robotic Mowers**: Automated grass court mowing
- **Automated Cleaners**: Surface cleaning for all court types
- **AI-Driven Irrigation**: Real-time water management based on conditions
- **Drone Monitoring**: Aerial court condition assessment
- **Maintenance Flagging**: Autonomous issue detection

#### Vertical Farm Operations
- **Automated Hydroponics**: Nutrient delivery and pH control
- **Climate Control**: Temperature, humidity, CO2 optimization
- **Robotic Transport**: Grass patch movement and placement
- **Growth Monitoring**: AI-powered plant health assessment

#### Building Management System (BMS)
- **Lighting Control**: Automated scheduling and energy optimization
- **HVAC Management**: Climate control across all floors
- **Energy Management**: Solar panel integration and load balancing
- **Real-Time Monitoring**: Sensor data streaming to digital twin

#### Player Management
- **Mobile App**: Booking, payments, court allocation
- **Biometric Access**: Secure entry points
- **Dynamic Scheduling**: AI-optimized court availability
- **Queue Management**: Real-time waiting area optimization

#### Emergency Systems
- **Automated Monitoring**: 24/7 incident detection
- **Emergency Services Alert**: Instant notification system
- **Evacuation Management**: Automated guidance systems
