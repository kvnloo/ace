## Component Breakdown

### ENTRANCE ZONE
- Width: 25m
- Signage: 8m × 1.2m LED panel (Yellow)
- Doors: 6m wide × 4m high (double doors)
- Handles: Metallic finish on both doors
- Clear text: "MEN'S LOCKER ROOM" / "WOMEN'S LOCKER ROOM"

### CHANGING AREA (Front Half)
- Dimensions: 25m × 7.5m ≈ 187.5m²
- 4 Locker Banks: 1.2m wide × 6m deep × 4m high
- 4 Benches: 1.5m wide × 6m deep wooden seating
- Windows: 6 units (1.5m × 2m each) for natural light
- Side Storage: 3m deep equipment closets on both sides
- Lighting: 2 overhead point lights

### SHOWER AREA (Back Half)
- Dimensions: 25m × 7.5m ≈ 112.5m²
- 8 Shower Stalls: 2.5m × 3m each (2 rows of 4)
- Privacy Walls: 4m high semi-transparent dividers
- Shower Heads: Individual fixtures in each stall
- Floor: Slip-resistant tile pattern
- Lighting: Dedicated overhead lighting
- Emergency Exit: Illuminated green sign

### STORAGE CLOSETS
- Location: Both sides of shower area
- Dimensions: 3m × 7.5m each ≈ 22.5m² per side
- Total Storage: ~45m² per facility
- Access: Door handles for equipment retrieval
- Purpose: Towels, cleaning supplies, maintenance equipment

## Spatial Calculations

### Total Area Breakdown
- Main Building: 25m × 15m = 375m² per facility
- Changing Area: 23m × 7.5m ≈ 172.5m²
- Shower Area: 23m × 7.5m ≈ 172.5m²
- Storage: 6m × 7.5m ≈ 45m² (combined both sides)
- Circulation: ~30m² (pathways, entry area)

### Occupancy Capacity (Estimated)
- Locker Spaces: 24 lockers × 4 banks = 96 lockers
- Bench Seating: ~20 simultaneous users
- Shower Capacity: 8 simultaneous users
- Total Design Capacity: ~25-30 concurrent users

## 3D Positioning

### East Locker Room (Men's)
- Position: [57.5, 0, 0]
- Rotation: 90° (π/2 radians)
- Entry Faces: West (toward facility center)
- Adjacent To: East side tennis courts

### West Locker Room (Women's)
- Position: [-57.5, 0, 0]
- Rotation: -90° (-π/2 radians)
- Entry Faces: East (toward facility center)
- Adjacent To: West side tennis courts

### Exterior
- Main Walls: Slate Blue (#1e293b) - matte finish
- Roof: Dark Slate (#0f172a) - low roughness
- Doors: Medium Gray (#334155) - metallic finish
- Signage: Brand Yellow (#DFFF4F) - emissive

### Interior
- Floor Tile: Light Gray (#e2e8f0) - low roughness
- Changing Area: Medium Gray (#475569) - semi-transparent
- Shower Area: Dark Gray (#334155) - semi-transparent
- Privacy Walls: Light Blue-Gray (#cbd5e1) - 60% opacity
- Benches: Brown Wood (#8b4513) - textured
- Lockers: Blue-Gray Metal (#64748b) - metallic
- Storage: Dark Slate (#1e293b) - solid

## Integration Points

### Building Connection
- Both facilities positioned at ±57.5m from center
- 15m width fits within building perimeter
- Entry doors face central court area
- Emergency exits face building exterior

### Accessibility
- Wide entry doors (6m) accommodate wheelchair access
- Clear circulation paths in changing area
- Emergency exits clearly marked and lit
- Natural light through multiple windows
- Slip-resistant flooring throughout

### Facility Access
- Positioned for equal access from all court areas
- Separate facilities maintain privacy standards
- Clear signage visible from main facility areas
- Direct pathways from courts to changing areas
